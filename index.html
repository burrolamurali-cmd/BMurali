1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "GP Data for PS" Android application (the "App") handles information. The App is a working tool for officials of the Panchayat Raj system of Telangana, India. It helps a Panchayat Secretary record household survey data, maintain the Gram Panchayat Cash Book, and maintain House Tax registers (Irusalunama, Demand-Collection-Balance register and monthly abstract), and it helps higher officers (Mandal, District and State level) to manage who may use the App for which area.

The App is not intended for the general public. Only officials whose mobile numbers have been authorised by their supervising officer can log in. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary in plain words:
  • Household survey records, Cash Book entries and House Tax entries are stored only on the user's own phone, in an encrypted database. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.
  • Only a small amount of administrative information is stored in the cloud (Google Firebase): the login mobile number, the officer hierarchy (who is DPO / MPDO / Secretary of which area), data-entry freeze switches, and month lock / unlock status.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not sell or rent personal information.

3. Information we handle

3.1 Login information (Firebase Authentication)

To log in, a user enters his or her mobile number. Google Firebase Phone Authentication sends a one-time password (OTP) by SMS and verifies it. The mobile number is processed and stored by Firebase Authentication as part of the login account. The App does not read your SMS inbox; you type the code yourself.

3.3 Household survey data (stored only on the device)

A Panchayat Secretary can record the following for each house and family of his or her Gram Panchayat, as part of official work:

  • House details: house or assessment number, owner and building details, building usage and structure, house tax paid information (receipt number, date and amount), water connection, electricity connection and meter numbers, toilet (IHHL) and soak pit details, solid waste management details.
  • Family details: ration card number, PPB (pattadar passbook) number, job card number (VBGRAM(G) / MGNREGA), family mobile number, wet land and dry land extent with survey numbers, gas connection company and number.
  • Family member details: name, relation, gender, age or date of birth, caste category and caste name, education, occupation, Aadhaar number, and pension (Cheyutha) type.

This data is entered by the Panchayat Secretary about residents of the Gram Panchayat. It is stored in an encrypted database on the phone and is not transmitted to the developer or uploaded to any cloud service by the App.

3.4 Cash Book data (stored only on the device)

Receipts and payments of the Gram Panchayat, fund heads and books, particulars, amounts, dates, month-wise balances, and the names of the Sarpanch, Upa-Sarpanch and Panchayat Secretaries entered for the reports. Stored in a separate encrypted database on the phone.

3.5 House Tax data (stored only on the device)

The house assessment list that the Panchayat Secretary uploads from a file downloaded from the official Panchayat website (assessment number, house number, owner name, father or husband name, mobile number, house tax, library cess and other tax demand, arrears), together with the Irusalunama receipts, cancelled receipt numbers, receipt book settings, DCB figures and monthly abstracts prepared in the App. The chosen file is read on the phone only; the App does not send it anywhere. This data is stored in a separate encrypted database on the phone.

3.6 Files created by the App

PDF reports, Word (RTF) files, Excel (CSV) files and House Tax backup files (JSON) are created inside the App's own storage area on the phone. They leave the phone only when the user chooses to share them (for example through WhatsApp, e-mail or Google Drive). Backup files are plain files and are not encrypted, so users should keep them safe and delete them when they are no longer needed.

3.7 Crash information

If the App crashes, a technical error message may be saved on the phone and shown on a screen so that the user can copy it. It is not sent automatically. It is shared only if the user decides to send it to the developer for troubleshooting.

3.8 What the App does not collect

  • No device location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera, microphone, call logs or SMS inbox.
  • No advertising ID, no analytics or usage tracking, no behavioural profiling.
  • No payment card or bank account details.

5. How we use the information

  • To verify that a person is an authorised official and to show the correct screens for his or her role and area.
  • To let officers authorise the next level of officers.
  • To apply and enforce data-entry freeze and month-lock rules, and to let only authorised officers unlock.
  • To let the Panchayat Secretary prepare official registers and reports (survey register, Cash Book, Irusalunama, DCB and monthly abstract) on the phone.
  • To show a record of who unlocked a month and why.

We do not use the information for advertising, marketing, profiling or any purpose unrelated to Panchayat administration.

6. Aadhaar numbers and other sensitive information

The survey form allows a Panchayat Secretary to record the Aadhaar number, caste, education, occupation and pension details of family members. This is entered only when the official survey requires it. The App does not verify Aadhaar numbers with UIDAI, does not store them in the cloud, and does not share them with the developer. They exist only in the encrypted database on the phone and in the reports that the Secretary chooses to export. Officials who export or share such reports are responsible for handling them in line with the applicable law and their department's instructions.

7. Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • Administrative data in section 3.2 is visible to signed-in users of the App as explained in section 9, and is processed by Google as our service provider.
  • Survey, Cash Book and House Tax data leave the phone only when the user exports and shares a report or backup file.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or by a lawful order of a competent authority.

8. Third-party services

The App uses only the following external services, both provided by Google LLC:

  • Firebase Authentication (phone number sign-in with SMS OTP).
  • Cloud Firestore (administrative data listed in section 3.2).

These services process data under Google's own terms and privacy documents: firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy. Google may process this data on servers located outside India. The App contains no other third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

9. Security

  • Encryption at rest: the survey, Cash Book and House Tax databases on the phone are encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). The database passphrase and the login session are kept using the Android Keystore based secure storage.
  • Encryption in transit: communication with Firebase uses HTTPS / TLS.
  • Access rules in the cloud: Firebase security rules allow only signed-in users to read administrative data and allow changes only by the proper officer. For example, a Panchayat Secretary can only lock a month, and only the DPO of the district can unlock a House Tax month.
  • Role-based login: only mobile numbers authorised by the higher officer can log in for an area.
  • No device backup: the App disables Android automatic cloud backup, so its local databases are not copied to Google Drive by the operating system.
  • Freeze and lock controls: locked months cannot be edited, and unlocking is recorded with a reason.

Because survey, Cash Book and House Tax records live on the phone, users should protect the phone with a screen lock, keep the App and the phone updated, and take House Tax backups regularly. If a phone is lost, reset or the App is uninstalled, data that has not been backed up cannot be recovered by the developer. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data retention

  • On the phone: data stays until the user deletes it or uninstalls the App. Uninstalling removes the local databases.
  • In the cloud: officer mappings, freeze switches, lock status and unlock log are kept for as long as they are needed for Panchayat administration, and are removed on request as described in section 11.

11. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your account or of the administrative data held in the cloud about you:

  1. Send an e-mail to burrolamurali@gmail.com from your own address, stating your name, your role, the area (district, mandal, Gram Panchayat) and the mobile number used to log in.
  2. We will remove your login account and your mobile number from the mappings, or ask the higher officer or State Admin to do so, and will confirm by reply.
  3. Data stored on your phone (survey, Cash Book and House Tax records, exported files) is under your control: delete records in the App, delete the exported files, or uninstall the App.

Please note that records that are part of official government registers may have to be retained by the Gram Panchayat as required by law and departmental instructions, even after a user's login is removed.
